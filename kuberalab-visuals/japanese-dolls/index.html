From collection
to living story

The project is built around the user's own Japanese dolls, collected in Thailand. Every post follows one controlled process: identify carefully, animate respectfully, write clearly and verify before publishing.

01

Identify

Classify the doll—such as Hina Ningyō, Obina or Mebina—without presenting an uncertain identification as fact.

02

Animate

Create a continuous seven-second runway-style sequence. The original doll begins moving immediately; there is no duplicated still-image opening.

03

Publish

Prepare an elegant English caption, a cultural note, a follow call-to-action and no more than five relevant global hashtags.

Three Reels formats

One consistent visual language, adapted to the number of dolls in the original photograph.

壱

Single Doll Runway

The doll rises slowly, walks forward like a ceremonial model and remains recognisably a traditional collectible doll.

弐

Imperial Pair

Two dolls rise together and advance in synchronised movement, preserving the exact relationship and composition of the source.

参

Collection Ensemble

Several dolls come alive together without adding, removing or duplicating any character from the original photograph.

creative rules

These constraints protect the identity of the collection and prevent the common AI errors that make the result feel artificial.

7-second Japanese runway

Create a 7-second vertical 9:16 cinematic video.

IMPORTANT OPENING RULE:
Do not display the uploaded photograph as a separate frozen opening frame. Do not create a photo-to-video cut, double image, duplicated doll, ghost copy or second version of the character. From the very first frame, the original doll or dolls are already present in their exact original positions and begin moving naturally inside one continuous scene.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
Keep the same doll identity, face, expression, hairstyle, headpiece, clothing, fabric patterns, colours, accessories, body proportions and exact number of characters.

ACTION:
The doll or dolls slowly rise from their original positions and walk gracefully forward like ceremonial Japanese runway models. Movement is elegant, controlled and synchronised where more than one doll is present.

CAMERA:
One continuous ultra-smooth backward tracking shot at chest height. No cuts, no sudden zoom, no rotation and no identity change.

BACKGROUND:
A refined Japanese interior with warm cinematic light, polished wood, restrained gold details and tasteful traditional motifs.

WALL TITLE:
Display “[DOLL NAME IN ENGLISH]” with “[VERIFIED JAPANESE NAME]” beneath it as a stable part of the rear wall. Do not place text over the doll.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
No duplicated characters, no extra dolls, no frozen-photo opening, no morphing, no face or costume change, no missing accessories, no extra limbs, no flicker, no text errors, no unrelated logos, no subtitles, no narration and no scene cuts.
